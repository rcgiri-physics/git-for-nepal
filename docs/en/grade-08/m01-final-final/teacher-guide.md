# Teacher guide — Module 1

**Timing:** 40-minute period. Hook 5 · folder game 15 · predict 10 · key idea 5 · exit 5.
**Materials:** printed paragraph (6 lines, any Nepali text, e.g. a short school notice), blank A4 sheets, pens. No computer needed.

## Common errors
- Students start the game by editing the original sheet. Remind them: *never change an old sheet, make a new one.*
- Students say "just use Ctrl+Z". Answer: undo disappears when you close the file; history must survive.

## Fallbacks
Same in all tiers; this lesson is fully unplugged.

## Answer key
- Hook: no way to know without opening every file.
- Predict: 30 × 5 × 6 = 900. Two people editing the same file produces two files with different edits that must be combined by hand.
- Exit 1: any of unclear order, no reason recorded, easy to overwrite, takes storage. 2: what, when, why. 3: False — names give no reason, no difference and can still be confused.

## Extension quest
Ask students to find a real `final_final` in their own folders at home and bring a description (not the file) tomorrow.
