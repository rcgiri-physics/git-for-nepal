# 8. Team capstone

## Hook
A client (a local organisation, with consent) needs a small tool. Your team of 3–4 (CDC's software-project unit allows at most four members) has 6+ weeks.

## Concept (unplugged or visual first)
Teams write: charter, branching strategy, review rule, definition of done, risk list.

## PRIMM lab
**Options:** (a) a **data repo** with a data card and CI that validates the CSV on every PR; (b) a web or Python app (OOP syllabus link); (c) a documentation or translation contribution to a partner project.
Requirements: ≥ 20 issues, PRs with reviews, CI green, ≥ 2 tagged releases, `README`, `CONTRIBUTING`, `CHANGELOG`, licence, no secrets, no personal data.
**Milestones:** week 1 plan · 2 skeleton + CI · 3–4 features · 5 hardening + security review · 6 release + demo + viva.

## Parsons practice
Order the weekly cycle: *plan issues · branch · PR · review · CI · merge · demo*.

## Exit ticket
1. What was your hardest decision and why?
2. How does the history show each member's work?
3. *Check:* what would you do differently?
