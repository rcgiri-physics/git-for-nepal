# 1. Open-source licences and attribution

## Hook
You find a great photo, a poem and a program online. Can you use them in your school project? What must you do?

## Concept (unplugged or visual first)
Card sort: licence cards (`MIT`, `CC BY 4.0`, `CC BY-SA 4.0`, `CC BY-NC-SA`, 'all rights reserved') vs. permissions (copy, change, sell, must credit, must share alike). Key: **no licence = no permission.** Open code → MIT; open writing/data → CC BY.

## PRIMM lab
1. Read this repo's `LICENSE-CONTENT.md` and `ATTRIBUTION.md`. **Predict** what you must write if you reuse Grade 8 Module 1.
2. Write attribution lines for three given items (a photo CC BY, a MIT library, a CC BY-SA poem — what changes for SA?).
3. **Investigate:** why can't a CC BY-NC-SA text be copied into this repo? (Conflicting terms.)
4. **Make:** choose a licence for your own Grade 11 project and add the file.

## Parsons practice
Match each licence to what it requires (credit / share alike / no commercial use).

## Exit ticket
1. What must you do when reusing CC BY work?
2. What does 'share alike' require?
3. *Misconception:* 'if it is on the internet, it is free to use.' True or false?

## Teacher notes
This is general information, not legal advice; avoid giving legal opinions. Link to the official summaries at creativecommons.org and opensource.org.
